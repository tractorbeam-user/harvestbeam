Opening it the first time

Harvestbeam isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS asks you to allow it once.

  1. Open Harvestbeam. When macOS says it can't verify it, click Done.
  2. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down.
  3. Click Open Anyway next to Harvestbeam and confirm with your password.

That's it. Updates install and open on their own after this.
